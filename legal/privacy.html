We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is only shared with trusted service providers (payment processors, email platforms) under confidentiality agreements.

We use essential, analytics, and affiliate tracking cookies. See our Cookie Policy for full details.

Data is retained as long as needed to provide services. Purchase records are kept 7 years for legal purposes. You may request deletion at any time.

You have the right to access, correct, delete, and port your data. Visit our Data Removal page or contact us.
