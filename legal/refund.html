Refund Policy

Last updated: April 24, 2026

✅ 14-Day Money Back Guarantee. If you're not satisfied within 14 days of purchase, we'll issue a full refund — no questions asked.

How to Request

Email support@recurhub.net with subject "Refund Request – [Your Order ID]" or use our Contact page. Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days.

What's Covered

  • All purchases within the 14-day window.
  • No reason required — all requests honoured.
  • One refund per customer per product.

What's Not Covered

  • Requests after 14 days from purchase.
  • Agency + Reseller licences with active resale or provisioned client accounts.
  • Third-party costs (autoresponder fees, custom domain registration, etc.).

Chargebacks

Please contact us before initiating a chargeback. Fraudulent chargebacks result in licence revocation.
