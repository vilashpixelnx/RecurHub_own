Data Removal Request

Last updated: April 24, 2026

Under GDPR, CCPA and applicable privacy laws, you have the right to request deletion of your personal data. We process all requests within 30 days.

What We Can Remove

  • Name and email from marketing lists.
  • Support ticket history.
  • Analytics profiles linked to your sessions.

What We Must Retain

  • Purchase records (7-year legal requirement).
  • Data needed to honour active licences or process refunds.
  • Fraud prevention records.

Submit a Request

If you have to have your data removed please open a ticket at

https://recurhub.net/support

We will take care of your request asap.
